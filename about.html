My story

Learning how businesses really work — through the people in them.

I am Pooja Mishra, a Bachelor of Business Administration student at GH Raisoni Institute of Commerce and Management, Saikheda. My course has given me a broad view of how organisations are run — from planning and communication to the way teams are built and led.

What draws me most is the human side of management. How does a company find the right person for a role? How does a new joiner feel welcome from day one? What makes an employee stay, grow and perform? These questions are at the heart of Human Resource Management, and I enjoy studying them.

At the same time, Product Management fascinates me because it asks a different but connected question: how do we understand what people need and build something useful for them? Learning both sides helps me think about business in a balanced way — people and product, together.

I believe in being honest about where I am: a fresher with no formal internship yet, but with a strong base of knowledge, practical office skills, and a work ethic that I am keen to put into real projects.
