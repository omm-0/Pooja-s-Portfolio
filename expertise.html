Field of focus — 01

Human Resource
Management

HR is the function that looks after an organisation's most valuable asset — its people. These are the areas I am studying and keen to practise.

01

Recruitment & Selection

How companies plan manpower, write clear job descriptions, source candidates, interview fairly and select the right person for the right role.

02

Onboarding & Induction

Helping new joiners settle in with the right information, introductions and expectations so they can contribute with confidence from the start.

03

Training & Development

Identifying skill gaps and supporting employee growth through training, mentoring and clear career paths.

04

Performance Management

Setting goals, giving constructive feedback and appraising performance in a way that is objective, transparent and motivating.

05

Employee Engagement

Understanding what keeps people motivated — recognition, communication, culture and work-life balance — and how engagement affects retention.

06

Organisational Development

Looking at structure, culture and change management to help organisations adapt and improve over time.

Field of focus — 02

Product
Management

Product thinking trains me to start with the user and work backwards — a mindset that is just as useful in HR as it is in building products.

Step 01

Understand the problem

Observe, ask questions and define who the user is and what real need is being solved, before thinking about solutions.

Step 02

Think through design

Use design-thinking methods — empathise, define, ideate, prototype and test — to explore several ideas instead of jumping to one.

Step 03

Analyse and prioritise

Use basic business analysis and Excel to compare options, understand numbers and decide what matters most.

Step 04

Work with a team

Coordinate with different people, communicate clearly and keep everyone aligned around a common goal.
